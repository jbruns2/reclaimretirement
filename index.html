What is Reclaim Your Retirement?

Each day, thousands of Hoosiers run the risk of forgetting their retirement benefits earned through public service.

The Indiana Public Retirement System (INPRS) wants to connect with members who may have forgotten their benefits or need to contact us to update their profiles.

A forgotten address change, an outdated email address, and a locked-out login later, and suddenly life gets so busy that you forget to remember your retirement benefit. And if enough years go by, you could become eligible to retire and not even know it.

Search our secure database by entering your first and last name and date of birth. If there’s a match, we’ll let you know that you need to contact us. If there’s not a match, you may still have a retirement benefit with us but you’re just not on our list for this initiative. If you’re not sure, please call us. Our team is ready to help you Reclaim Your Retirement so you can realize your retirement dreams.

Who Should Search the Database?

If you ever worked in a public service role in the state of Indiana, like in a government office, school, or as a first responder, you may have a retirement account and benefits waiting for you..

Individuals

Search your name to discover your forgotten retirement account.

Employers

Search current or former employee names to help them reunite with their forgotten accounts.

Financial Planners

Search client names to help clients connect with INPRS to reclaim their retirement benefits.
